About

A university-run week inside a global festival.

Qiskit Fall Fest is an annual season of student-run quantum computing events supported by IBM Quantum. This is the BITS Pilani edition.

The festival

What Qiskit Fall Fest is

Each autumn, IBM Quantum backs student organisers at universities around the world to run their own quantum computing events — workshops, lectures, hackathons — using a shared identity and a shared set of teaching materials.

There is no single central event. Every campus builds its own programme, which is why this one is shaped around what BITS Pilani teaches and who is here to teach it.

The host

Why Pilani

Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani sits at the intersection this programme needs: physics and computer science departments that already share students, and faculty working on quantum information.

The week is written for undergraduates who can code but have not yet met a qubit. It starts at the prerequisites and does not assume a physics major.

What the week covers

Six threads, in order

  • FoundationsLinear algebra, probability and statistics, quantum mechanics
  • QubitsSingle-qubit states, measurement, Bell states and entanglement
  • Circuits & algorithmsGate-level circuit construction, Grover's and friends
  • Variational & QMLVariational quantum circuits, then a full quantum machine learning workshop
  • CryptographyClassical crypto foundations, then quantum key distribution hands-on
  • PhotonicsPhotonic approaches to quantum information, with a guest lecture
