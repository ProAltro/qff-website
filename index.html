GLOBAL INITIATIVE

What is Qiskit Fall Fest?

Qiskit Fall Fest is an international celebration of quantum computation sponsored by IBM Quantum. Designed for undergraduate, graduate, and high-school innovators, it delivers a university-tailored festival format featuring hands-on technical workshops, direct access to cloud quantum hardware, and real-world algorithmic challenges.

Participants explore the cutting edge with Qiskit 1.0 and Qiskit Runtime Primitives (Estimator & Sampler), learning how to write scalable quantum circuits that mitigate noise and prepare for quantum utility.

INSTITUTE OF EMINENCE

Hosted by BITS Pilani

Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani (Pilani Campus) is recognized as one of India's premier technological and research universities with an illustrious history of engineering excellence.

The Pilani campus spearheads interdisciplinary quantum research across theoretical physics, quantum information, and high-performance quantum algorithms. By hosting Qiskit Fall Fest 2026, BITS Pilani connects collegiate developers across the globe with IBM Quantum scientists and hardware backends.

CAMPUS SPOTLIGHT

Quantum Computing at BITS Pilani

The Department of Physics and Computer Science at BITS Pilani fosters a vibrant quantum ecosystem. From student-led research in fault-tolerant quantum error correction to tensor network simulations and quantum machine learning, the institute provides a launchpad for deep-tech innovation.

Quantum Information Theory Investigating entanglement witnesses, quantum discord, and non-local correlations.
Superconducting Hardware Control Optimizing microwave pulse sequences and dynamic circuit execution on IBM QPUs.
Quantum Machine Learning Exploring parameterized variational quantum circuits for chemistry and optimization.
